# Contributing to Webmote

Thanks for your interest in Webmote.

## Issues are welcome

Bug reports and feature ideas are very welcome: open an issue. For a bug, please include:

- your TV's model and webOS version (TV settings → General → About this TV)
- your phone's model and Android version
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

## Pull requests aren't accepted yet

Webmote is developed by one person, and pull requests can't be merged for now. If you've found a
fix, please describe it in an issue instead: it will be credited in the change that lands it.
