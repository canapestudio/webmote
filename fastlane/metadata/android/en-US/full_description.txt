Webmote turns your phone into a fast, private remote for LG TVs running webOS (2018 models and later). Open it and press a button, or don't open it at all: use a Quick Settings tile, a home-screen widget or your phone's volume keys.

<b>Everything on one screen</b>
• D-pad with OK, Back, Home, Settings and Info
• Volume slider that follows the TV's real level, with mute and ±
• Brightness slider (backlight, or OLED light on OLED sets)
• Play/pause, stop, rewind and fast-forward
• Input picker (HDMI 1, 2, 3…)
• 123 pad with channels and the red, green, yellow and blue keys
• Live keyboard: type on your phone and the text appears on the TV as you type

<b>Picture settings</b>
Adjust brightness, contrast, colour and energy saving from a Picture sheet that shows the TV's current values. If your TV ignores picture changes, Webmote greys the controls out and tells you why instead of pretending they work.

<b>Touchpad and apps</b>
Move the TV's pointer like a Magic Remote, tap to click and scroll with two fingers. Launch Netflix, YouTube and the rest from a grid of your TV's apps, and pin your favourites to the top.

<b>Without opening the app</b>
• Quick Settings tiles: Power, Mute, Screen off (sound keeps playing) and Open remote
• Two home-screen widgets: a 4×1 strip and a 4×2 mini remote
• A notification with −, mute, + and power while the TV is on
• Your phone's volume keys change the TV's volume, even with the phone locked (you can turn this off)

<b>Turn the TV on</b>
When the TV is off, one tap on Turn on wakes it with Wake-on-LAN, and the remote appears as soon as it answers.

<b>Several TVs</b>
Save every TV in the house, rename them ("Living room", "Bedroom") and switch between them from the top bar.

<b>Private by design</b>
No account, no analytics. Webmote talks only to your TVs on your own network. Each TV's security certificate is recorded when you pair it, so another device can't pretend to be your TV.

<b>Requirements</b>
• An LG TV with webOS 4 or later (2018 models onwards), on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone
• To turn the TV on from your phone, enable "Turn on via Wi-Fi" (Settings → General → Mobile TV On) or, on 2025 models and later, Wake on LAN (Support → IP control settings)
